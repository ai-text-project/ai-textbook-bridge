**Repository:** ACIM Structural Representation Repository

**Reference Layer**

**Version:** v1.0 (Provisional)

# Purpose

This document validates the terminology employed throughout the Structural Representation Repository.

Unlike the Validation Observation, which records structural observations obtained from independent reference resources, this document evaluates whether the terminology adopted in each repository asset preserves the same structural responsibility identified in the Wapnick Glossary.

Validation is therefore performed at the level of structural responsibility rather than lexical correspondence.

---

# Validation Method

Validation proceeds in the same order as the repository itself.

Each repository asset is independently validated against the Wapnick Glossary.

The objective is not to reproduce glossary terminology.

The objective is to confirm that the organizational responsibility assigned to repository terminology is consistently supported by the reference resource.

# Validation Principle

Terminology is evaluated together with its structural responsibility.

Accordingly, consistency is determined not merely by lexical agreement, but by the preservation of organizational role within the Structural Representation Repository.

---

# Completion Criterion

Terminology Validation is completed when every repository asset has been independently validated against the Wapnick Glossary and no unresolved terminology inconsistencies remain.
