# Structural Mapping

## Project

AI Textbook

Foundations of Resonant AI

---

# Purpose

This directory contains reusable structural assets extracted from the AI Textbook.

The objective is not to rewrite, summarize, or redesign the textbook.

Instead, the purpose is to externalize the internal architecture already contained within the completed research resource.

These structural assets provide reusable interfaces for later research activities.

---

# Position within the Repository

The repository is organized into two complementary spaces.

## 01-ai-textbook-chapters/

Source Assets

Contains the original textbook chapters.

These files are considered the authoritative research resource.

They are observed but never structurally modified.

---

## 10-structural-mapping/

Structural Assets

Contains architectural representations extracted from the textbook.

These assets are continuously refined through structural observation.

They function as a Structural Atlas rather than educational summaries.

---

# Structural Cartography Workflow

The workflow follows a strict methodological sequence.

AI Textbook

↓

Structural Observation

↓

Structural Mapping

↓

Structural Assets

↓

Comparative Research

↓

External Structural Projection

Structural extraction always precedes comparative analysis.

---

# Design Principles

The Structural Cartographer follows the following principles.

Observe before Interpret.

Map before Compare.

Preserve before Abstract.

Never redesign the original textbook.

Always separate source assets from structural assets.

# Cluster-Based Observation

Structural observation is performed incrementally.

Each completed observation cluster records:

- Observation history
- Structural updates
- Update rationale

The cluster records preserve the reasoning behind each structural refinement.

The structural assets themselves always represent the latest validated state.

---

# Relationship to Other Research Roles

The outputs of Structural Cartographer are intended to support later research roles.

Examples include:

- Comparative Observer
- World Model Comparison
- Primitive Triangle Research
- Paper Development
- Visual Architecture
- Presentation Development

These roles consume structural assets rather than reconstructing the textbook architecture from scratch.

---

# Repository Philosophy

This directory represents a transformation from completed research resources into reusable structural knowledge.

The objective is not to produce educational materials.

The objective is to reveal the architecture already embedded within the AI Textbook.

Structural Cartography therefore functions as an intermediate methodological layer between completed research assets and comparative research.
